1. Upper Body

Muscles: chest, back, shoulders

Exercises: push-ups, bench press, pull-ups, shoulder press, rows

2. Lower Body

Muscles: quadriceps, hamstrings, glutes, calves

Exercises: squats, lunges, deadlifts, leg press, calf raises

3. Core / Abs

Muscles: abs, obliques, lower back

Exercises: planks, crunches, Russian twists, leg raises, back extensions

4. Arms

Muscles: biceps, triceps, forearms

Exercises: bicep curls, tricep dips, hammer curls, tricep pushdowns

5. Legs

Muscles: quadriceps, hamstrings, calves, glutes (can overlap with lower body)

Exercises: squats, lunges, calf raises, hamstring curls

6. Shoulders

Muscles: deltoids, traps

Exercises: shoulder press, lateral raises, front raises, shrugs

7. Back

Muscles: lats, traps, rhomboids, erector spinae

Exercises: pull-ups, rows, deadlifts, lat pull-downs
